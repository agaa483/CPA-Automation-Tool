Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how QB Auditor ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our software and services (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and authentication credentials via our identity provider (Clerk). We do not store passwords.

Connected account tokens. When you authorize the Service to access your QuickBooks Online or Microsoft Outlook accounts, we store OAuth access and refresh tokens. These tokens are used only to fetch data on your behalf and are never shared.

QuickBooks data. We access transaction data, chart of accounts, and vendor information from your connected QuickBooks Online companies. We store transaction metadata (ID, date, amount, vendor, category) and audit results locally in our database.

Email content. We access emails from your connected Outlook mailbox, filtered by sender addresses you configure. Email content is fetched at audit time and passed to our AI provider (Anthropic Claude) for evidence matching. We do not permanently store email bodies.

Uploaded files. When you upload spreadsheets for audit, we process them and generate annotated output. Original and processed files are stored temporarily and deleted after your session.

Audit logs. We store a complete record of every audit decision, including the prompt sent to our AI provider, the AI's response, and the final decision. This supports auditability and debugging.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core Service (auditing transaction categorizations);
  • To communicate with you about your account or the Service;
  • To debug issues and improve the Service;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data to train AI models or sell your data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

The Service relies on the following third-party providers to function. Data may be transmitted to these providers as required to deliver the Service:

  • Intuit QuickBooks Online — for transaction and account data. Governed by Intuit's privacy policy.
  • Microsoft Graph (Outlook) — for email evidence. Governed by Microsoft's privacy statement.
  • Anthropic Claude — for AI categorization decisions. Governed by Anthropic's privacy policy. Anthropic does not train on data submitted via API.
  • Clerk — for user authentication. Governed by Clerk's privacy policy.
  • Fly.io — for backend hosting.
  • Vercel — for frontend hosting.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored in an encrypted SQLite database on a persistent volume hosted by Fly.io in the United States. OAuth tokens are stored in the database and used only to make authorized API calls to connected services. All connections between the Service and third-party APIs use HTTPS/TLS.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your firm's data (client records, OAuth tokens, audit logs, category lists) within 30 days. Audit log records may be retained longer if required by law.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Delete your information (subject to legal retention obligations);
  • Export your data;
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

8. International Users

The Service is hosted in the United States. By using the Service, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via the Service or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version.
